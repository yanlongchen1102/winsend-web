Intel Unison Is Discontinued: The Best Alternatives for iPhone

Intel Unison used to connect your iPhone to a Windows PC. It no longer works. Here is what happened, and what to use now for files, photos, messages and calls.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Quick Answer: Is Intel Unison Still Working?

No. Intel ended Intel Unison on June 30, 2025 and removed it from the Microsoft Store on July 1, 2025. On most PCs the app no longer works correctly and can't be downloaded. The only exception Intel announced was Lenovo Aura Edition PCs, which kept service through the rest of 2025.

To replace it on an iPhone, use Microsoft Phone Link for calls and text messages, and Phone Link, WinSend or LocalSend for files and photos. For copy and paste between iPhone and PC, which Phone Link doesn't support, use WinSend.

What Was Intel Unison?

Intel Unison was a free app, launched in 2022, that linked a Windows 11 PC with an iPhone or Android phone. Intel built it on technology from Screenovate, a company it bought in 2021. With an iPhone, Unison let you:

  • send files and photos between the phone and the PC;
  • read and reply to text messages;
  • make and answer calls;
  • see phone notifications on the PC.

Many iPhone users liked it because, at the time, it did more with an iPhone than Microsoft's own Phone Link.

Why Was Intel Unison Discontinued?

Intel announced the shutdown with an in-app notice in spring 2025 ("Intel Unison is approaching its sunset at the end of June 2025"). Reports linked the decision to Intel's large layoffs, which led to the Unison team being disbanded. PC makers such as HP and Dell confirmed the end of support for their devices.

The Alternatives in Detail

1. Microsoft Phone Link: the closest all-in-one replacement

Phone Link, together with the Link to Windows app on the iPhone, covers most of what Unison did: calls, single-person texts, notifications and, since 2025, file sharing in both directions. It is free and already on Windows 10 and 11. Its limits with an iPhone: no clipboard sync, no group messages, and messages only appear while the phone is connected over Bluetooth. Read our guide to Phone Link for iPhone.

2. WinSend: files, photos and copy-paste between iPhone and PC

WinSend focuses on what you move between the devices most: photos, videos, files and copied text. Send from the Photos share sheet straight to the PC, tap “Get from PC” to grab the PC clipboard, or double-tap the back of the iPhone (Back Tap) to send copied text to Windows. It works on Windows 10 and 11 over your local network, without Bluetooth or an account. Every feature is free to try; after that, WinSend Pro is a one-time purchase.

3. LocalSend: free and open source

LocalSend sends files between almost any devices on the same network, including iPhone and Windows. It is completely free and open source, but it doesn't handle calls, messages or the clipboard.

For more options, including browser-based tools, see the AirDrop alternatives for Windows.

Which One Should You Use?

  • You mainly used Unison for calls and texts → Microsoft Phone Link.
  • You mainly moved photos and files → Phone Link works; WinSend or LocalSend if you prefer sending over your local network.
  • You copy text between iPhone and PC every day → WinSend, alongside Phone Link if you also want calls.
